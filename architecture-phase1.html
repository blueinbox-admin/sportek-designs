Phase 1 · Internal Team Tool

Sportek AI Dashboard — Architecture

Three data sources, each feeding one chatbot inside a single branded internal website behind a staff login. Every bot is a Claude assistant wired to one source only through its own MCP connection. The bots never share or combine data, which removes the riskiest part of these builds while still delivering the must-have: sales by rep, style, and invoice.

Where the real work is Almost all of the effort lives in the QuickBooks bot: deciding and encoding how Sportek actually counts sales by rep — net of returns, credit memos, and discounts — then testing it against real questions until the numbers are trustworthy. That definition work is what no off-the-shelf tool does for you, and it's the difference between a bot that sounds confident and one the sales team can rely on.
Single-source by designEach bot answers only about its own system. We deliberately don't combine data across Shopify, QuickBooks, and HubSpot — the part that makes these projects fail.
No data centerThere's no shared warehouse or sync layer. Every bot connects live to its source through that source's MCP, then reasons over the result with Claude.
Internal only this phasePhase 1 is the staff dashboard. The customer-facing store assistant (Shopify Storefront + Customer accounts MCPs) is a later phase.
