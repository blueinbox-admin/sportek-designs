Sportek AI Internal Dashboard: Architecture

One branded website behind a staff login, with three AI assistants side by side: Shopify, QuickBooks, and HubSpot. Each connects to a single source and only answers about that source. They never combine data, which keeps it simple and safe while still delivering the must-have: sales by salesperson, style, and invoice.

Connecting the sources is mostly off the shelf. In Phase 1 each bot answers from its own source, and David starts capturing Sportek's rules and terms into a knowledge base as the team uses it. Feeding that knowledge back so every answer comes out in Sportek's terms, like "sales by rep" net of returns, credits, and discounts, and testing until the numbers are trustworthy, is Phase 2.

What it won't do Combine or map data across the three sources. Each bot only sees its own system, so a question that needs two of them can't be answered. For example, "How many of yesterday's online orders were Maria's?" needs Shopify orders linked to QuickBooks salespeople, so it's Phase 2.

Sportek AI Dashboard internal · staff login · three tabs
Tab 1 Shopify bot (click to see simple mockup)
  • Answers everyday questions about the online store: products, inventory, orders, and customers. Sliceable by date or product, and it can show the orders behind an answer.
  • Connected securely behind the staff login, with control over who sees what.
  • David confirms which storefronts it covers and talks to the team to learn how the store is set up. Capturing their product names and terms stays basic in Phase 1.
  • Read-only and in its lane: it only reads, never changes data, and says when it doesn't know instead of guessing.
  • Trained and tested against the team's real questions until answers are accurate; staff can flag anything off for David to fix.
Asks like
How many units of the Coastal Hoodie are in stock right now?
What sold the most online last month?
What's the status of order #10482?
Who are our top online customers this quarter?
Tab 2 QuickBooks bot (click to see simple mockup)
  • Answers the sales team's questions about sales, broken down by salesperson, style, and invoice. Sliceable by date, rep, style, or customer, and it can show the invoices behind a number.
  • Connected securely behind the staff login; Sportek's financial data stays private and is never used to train outside models.
  • Works with QuickBooks Online; if Sportek is on Desktop, that changes the approach, so confirm first.
  • The core work, and where David spends the most time: learning how sales are recorded, confirming the salesperson is captured on each sale, and pinning down exactly how Sportek counts a sale (net of returns, credits, and discounts), checked against their existing reports.
  • Read-only and in its lane: it only reads, never changes the books, and says when it doesn't know instead of guessing.
  • Tested the hardest, since the team relies on these numbers; staff can flag anything off for David to fix.
Asks like
What were Maria's net sales last month, after returns and credit memos?
How much did style 4471 sell across all reps this quarter?
Pull up invoice #5530, what's on it and was anything credited back?
Which rep had the highest net sales this season?
Tab 3 HubSpot bot (click to see simple mockup)
  • Answers the team's questions about the CRM: contacts, deals, and pipeline. Sliceable by date, deal owner, or stage, and it can show the deals behind an answer.
  • Connected securely behind the staff login.
  • David confirms what's actually in HubSpot and maps how the records and the team's own labels are organized.
  • Read-only and in its lane: it only reads, never changes records, and says when it doesn't know instead of guessing.
  • Trained and tested against the questions that matter; staff can flag anything off for David to fix.
Asks like
What deals are open for the Dick's account?
Which deals are set to close this month?
Who's our contact at Scheels?
What's the total value of the open pipeline right now?
